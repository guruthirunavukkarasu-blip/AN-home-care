Our Specialized Care Solutions

Comprehensive Care & Home Support

Every family has unique needs. Choose from our six dedicated home care and housekeeping services—available on flexible hourly, 12-hour, or 24-hour live-in schedules.

Child Care

Loving, attentive nannies and trained babysitters who ensure your newborn, infant, or toddler is safe, engaged, and well-nourished while you work or rest.

  • Newborn & infant feeding & hygiene
  • Toddler supervision & playtime
  • Verified & motherly female caretakers

Elder Care

Dignified companionship and daily living assistance for senior citizens, helping them age gracefully and comfortably in the familiarity of their own home.

  • Walking, mobility & fall-prevention support
  • Timely medication & meal reminders
  • Warm emotional companionship

Men & Women Care

Dedicated gender-matched male and female personal care assistants ensuring complete privacy, cultural sensitivity, and personal comfort for every individual.

  • Male caretakers for men; Female for women
  • Bathing, grooming & dressing assistance
  • Respectful, dignified personal hygiene

Patient Attendant

Specialized bedside and post-hospitalization recovery support for surgical, convalescing, or bedridden patients at home or in hospital wards.

  • Post-surgery & hospital discharge care
  • Bedridden turning, feeding & sponge bath
  • Vitals tracking & doctor visit accompaniment

Caregiver Service

Compassionate, trained full-time and part-time caregivers tailored for chronic conditions, disability support, and long-term family peace of mind.

  • 12-hour day/night & 24-hour live-in options
  • Dementia, stroke & paralysis home support
  • Immediate replacement guarantee

Housekeeping Services

Hygienic, reliable home cleaning, sanitization, and domestic assistance so your living space stays fresh, healthy, and stress-free every day.

  • Daily domestic help, sweeping & mopping
  • Kitchen, bathroom & patient-room sanitization
  • Trustworthy, police-verified housekeepers
